Fire detection and alarm system overhaul
01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Approval is sought for the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of a replacement addressable fire detection and alarm system at Ikeja City Mall. Following the review of the original tenders and the subsequent clarification submissions, Roundbox Solutions Services is ranked first, EMO-Technical Services second and JML/Fadayomi third. Roundbox is preferred because its proposal is supported by the only substantive post-inspection technical assessment, provides a complete TANDA system migration, includes direct engagement with the TANDA manufacturer and carries a stated three-year material warranty. The proposed award remains subject to formal acceptance of the change from a GENT-compatible overhaul to a complete TANDA replacement and the contract safeguards set out in this motivation.
02. BACKGROUND AND NEED
The existing mall fire alarm installation has been reported as damaged and non-functional and cannot presently be relied upon for fire detection, alarm initiation, occupant notification or system supervision. The original Request for Proposal contemplated the repair and upgrading of the existing GENT Vigilon installation. Five submissions were initially assessed and each bidder was asked to clarify equipment, system architecture, professional fees, certification, warranty and commercial terms. The revised submissions show that the immediate management decision is not merely which components to replace, but whether to retain the GENT platform or approve a complete migration to a new TANDA platform. Restoration of a dependable system is necessary for life safety, statutory compliance, asset protection and continuity of mall operations.
03. EVALUATION FINDINGS AND RANKING
01 Roundbox Solutions Services — ₦42,734,219.44 including VAT. Roundbox proposes a complete TANDA replacement rather than a partial GENT overhaul. Its submission is supported by a site-inspection report which states that the existing panels and field devices are damaged and non-functional and explains the proposed migration, loop capacity and integrated panel architecture. TANDA, the equipment manufacturer, is directly engaged and has committed technical support, facilities training and ongoing maintenance assistance. The quotation states a three-year warranty on materials, and TANDA's technical submission separately confirms a three-year warranty. Roundbox therefore offers the clearest single-source technical route from equipment manufacture through installation support. Its two-week execution period and materially lower price are additional strengths.
02 EMO-Technical Services — ₦87,802,775 including VAT. EMO submitted the strongest commercial particulars among the GENT-based bidders: 90-day validity, 30-day execution, a 12-month warranty and milestone payment terms of 50%, 30% and 20%. Its proposal covers the principal bill items and demonstrates stronger commercial organisation than the remaining GENT submission. It is ranked second because no equivalent technical assessment, loop-loading justification or manufacturer-certification evidence accompanied the revised quotation, and the price is ₦45,068,555.56 above Roundbox.
03 JML/Fadayomi — ₦76,137,950 including VAT. JML submitted the lower of the two revised GENT prices and corrected the earlier omission of the expansion panel. It remains commercially attractive. It is ranked third because it did not provide the requested technical assessment, GENT certification, delivery period, execution programme, payment terms or item-specific warranty information. Its network architecture, device variants and low containment and civil-works allowances also require further confirmation.
04 Warranty position. Roundbox states “three years on material”, while TANDA states that a three-year warranty is offered and that its team will continue to provide maintenance support. The available documents do not yet define the commencement date, workmanship coverage, exclusions, response time, replacement procedure, labour charges or local stock of critical spares. These details must be incorporated into the contract and warranty certificate before award.
05 Scope and procurement position. Roundbox's proposal is not a cheaper version of the originally tendered GENT overhaul; it is a different complete-system replacement. Its selection therefore requires a recorded management decision approving the revised technical basis. The TANDA technical assessment should be treated as vendor evidence and verified during pre-award due diligence, particularly because the report does not include test readings or an independent condition assessment.
04. RECOMMENDATION AND CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
It is recommended that Roundbox Solutions Services be selected as the preferred contractor, subject to the following conditions:

1. Management formally approves the change in scope from a GENT-compatible overhaul to a complete TANDA system replacement.
2. Roundbox and TANDA jointly confirm the final system design, loop-loading calculations, cause-and-effect matrix, device schedule, cable routes, interfaces and compliance certificates before mobilisation.
3. The three-year warranty runs from successful commissioning and handover. It must identify the warrantor, cover all supplied equipment, define exclusions and response times, and state the process for repair or replacement. Roundbox should also provide a minimum 12-month workmanship warranty.
4. TANDA issues a manufacturer support letter naming the local support contact, confirming training, commissioning support, spare-parts availability and maintenance assistance in Nigeria.
5. The contract sum of ₦42,734,219.44 is confirmed as inclusive of equipment, freight, clearing, taxes, local delivery, installation, containment, civil works, programming, testing, commissioning, training, documentation and handover, with exchange-rate and import-cost risk remaining with the contractor.
6. The quoted front-loaded payment terms are revised so that a meaningful balance is retained until successful testing, commissioning, training, delivery of as-built documents and handover. Any advance for imported materials should be supported by appropriate security and documentary evidence.
7. Installation is undertaken by competent personnel under a named responsible engineer, with test certificates, as-built drawings, operation manuals, asset registers and training records delivered at handover.
8. If Roundbox fails to satisfy these conditions, negotiations should proceed with EMO-Technical as the reserve bidder. JML remains the third-ranked option.
05. CONCLUSION
Roundbox offers the strongest overall motivation for award because it combines the lowest complete-project price, a stated technical basis for replacing the failed installation, direct access to the TANDA manufacturer, manufacturer-backed technical support and the longest stated material warranty. The recommendation is not based on price alone. It is based on the proposed complete restoration of the mall's fire detection capability and the support chain presented for the new system. Approval should therefore be granted subject to the scope-change decision, independent pre-award verification and incorporation of the commercial, technical and warranty protections stated above.
