PROJECT DESCRIPTION
This preliminary draft covers the proposed completion of the bar area at Engineer C. K. Musa’s existing residence in Afuze, Edo State. The existing photographs show a partially formed counter and opening with a visible entrance/right-hand access. The concept set proposes a warm timber-clad bar front, light stone countertop, internal storage and warm feature lighting. The two alternatives differ only by seating: Option 1 includes one bar stool; Option 2 includes no bar stool. Both options retain the existing architecture, entrance, surrounding walls, door, counter footprint and floor arrangement shown in the photographs; no new floor recess or geometry change is proposed. The bar area was not included in the initial works package and is presented as an additional scope for discussion. Final quantities, material selections, service requirements and rates remain subject to site measurement and approval. Attached concept visuals: BAR-2026-001_C-K-Musa_Afuze-Bar-Option-1-One-Stool.png and BAR-2026-002_C-K-Musa_Afuze-Bar-Option-2-No-Stool.png.
