PROJECT DESCRIPTION
This quotation covers the listed renovation and external improvement works at Engineer C. K. Musa’s residence in Afuze, Edo State. It includes the living-room expansion and associated alterations, BQ renovation and repairs, and access-road repair works. The quotation is offered as a discounted lump sum; detailed internal material and labour build-up is not included.
