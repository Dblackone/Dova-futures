PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Proposed remedial works for visible roof leakage at an Ikeja site. The scope is based solely on visible inspection and comprises two measures: Measure 1 — coping, blockwork and associated cracked-wall repairs, including local waterproofing and continuous roof-cap/coping corrections; and Measure 2 — precautionary localised screeding, priming and bituminous refelting at approximately 144 m² of slab-to-wall perimeter. Exact entry point and leakage path remain subject to opening-up and investigation when work begins. Full-roof screeding is excluded.
