1.0   PROJECT OVERVIEW
This report presents the design description, scope of works, preliminary Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and quotation for the construction of a reinforced-concrete swimming pool for Homework. The pool measures 5.4 m × 3.2 m on plan with a graduated floor from 1.0 m at the shallow end to 1.6 m at the deep end, finished in glass mosaic tiles and serviced by a sand-filter circulation system with underwater lighting. The pool is closely comparable in size and specification to the pool DOVA Futures recently completed at Ibafo (Ref: DFL/QT/2026/POOL-001); the same proven build-up rates have therefore been used as the basis of this quotation, with a modest location adjustment applied to reflect this site.
1.1   DESIGN DESCRIPTION
· Reinforced-concrete pool with corner entry steps for safe, gradual access into the pool.
· Graduated floor sloping from 1.0 m (shallow end) to 1.6 m (deep end).
· Concrete coping band wrapping the water body, with an adjoining paved deck / seating area.
· Recessed plant niche housing the sand filter and circulation pump, screened within the boundary wall zone.
· Finish: glass mosaic pool tiles (Client supply) over waterproofed render; matt coping and external walkway tiles.

2.0   SCOPE OF WORKS
· Civil works: setting out, excavation, blinding, reinforced-concrete pool base and walls, 9″ blockwork, rendering and waterproofing.
· Mechanical works: supply and installation of sand filter, circulation pump, suction/return pipework, skimmer and main-drain fittings.
· Electrical works: underwater pool lights, wiring, conduits and control-panel installation.
· Tiling works: laying of Client-supplied mosaic tiles to pool floor, walls and steps (labour only).
Civil materials and tiles are supplied by the Client (Section A). All labour, and the mechanical and electrical packages, are executed by Dova Futures Limited.
