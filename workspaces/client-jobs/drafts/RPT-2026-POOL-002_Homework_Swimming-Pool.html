01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report sets out the scope, technical basis and cost of constructing a swimming pool of 5.4 m × 3.2 m with a graduated depth of 1.0 m to 1.6 m for Homework. The pool is closely comparable in size and specification to DOVA Futures' completed FHS Hotel pool at Ibafo, so the same tried rates have been used as the pricing basis, with a modest location adjustment of approximately 14%. Including a 3% contingency, contractor's overheads & profit, full site supervision, and VAT at 7.5%, the recommended contract sum is ₦7,180,000 (Seven Million, One Hundred and Eighty Thousand Naira). We recommend proceeding on a 60/30/10 staged-payment basis.
02. BACKGROUND & POOL SPECIFICATION
Homework requested a costed proposal for a private reinforced-concrete swimming pool. The dimensions and depth profile are almost identical to a pool DOVA Futures recently delivered at Ibafo, allowing us to price from proven build-up rates rather than first-principles estimates. Key parameters of the proposed pool are:
Plan dimensions5.4 m × 3.2 m
Depth profile (shallow → deep)1.0 m → 1.6 m (graduated floor)
Water surface area≈ 17.3 m²
Approx. water volume≈ 22.5 m³ (avg. depth 1.3 m)
ConstructionReinforced concrete, rendered & waterproofed, tiled finish
FiltrationSand filter + circulation pump, skimmer & main-drain circuit
03. BASIS OF PRICING & COST BUILD-UP
01 Rates are taken directly from the FHS Ibafo pool of comparable size, then uplifted by ≈14% to reflect this site's location, access and logistics — the "slightly more" the principal directed.
02 The four works packages — Civil/Structural, Mechanical (filtration), Electrical, and Tiling (labour) — carry a 3% contingency and 10% contractor's overheads & profit, exactly as on the FHS job.

04. RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Adopt the contract sum of ₦7,180,000 (VAT and supervision inclusive) as set out in quote QTE-2026-POOL-002. 2. Structure payment as 60% mobilisation, 30% on completion of casting & waterproofing, and 10% on handover, mirroring the FHS arrangement. 3. Treat finish tiles as client-supplied, as priced. 4. Confirm site access and a clean water source before mobilisation, and agree in writing any scope beyond the pool shell (fencing, decking, landscaping), which is excluded and would be quoted as a separate variation.
05. CONCLUSION
Because the pool mirrors a job DOVA Futures has already built and priced at Ibafo, this estimate rests on proven rates rather than assumptions, giving strong confidence in the figures. The recommended contract sum of ₦7,180,000 — inclusive of a 14% location adjustment, contingency, overheads & profit, full site supervision and VAT — represents a fair, deliverable price. Figures assume normal ground conditions and unobstructed site access; any rock excavation, dewatering or abnormal founding would be handled as a priced variation.
