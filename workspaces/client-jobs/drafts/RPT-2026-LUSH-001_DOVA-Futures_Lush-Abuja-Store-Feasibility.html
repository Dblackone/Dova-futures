01. SUMMARY & PROJECT UNDERSTANDING
DOVA Futures Limited has reviewed the Lush Construction Materials Buying Guide (updated June 2026) in relation to a proposed store at Jabi Mall, Abuja. Based on the current requirements and a preliminary review of Nigerian supply routes, we consider the project feasible in principle. Most fabrication, installation and conventional construction work can be completed in Nigeria; selected brand-critical or certification-sensitive items should be imported. With early approvals, samples and procurement planning, the imported materials can be scheduled to arrive before the agreed store-opening date. This assessment is preliminary and is not a final bill of quantities, quotation or construction commitment.
02. LUSH ETHICAL & MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
The procurement basis is: prioritise reuse and refurbishment; use recycled, recyclable or rapidly renewable materials; use traceable responsibly sourced timber; and select low-VOC, cruelty-free, animal-free products with EPD or equivalent evidence where required. Materials and suppliers on the Lush red-flag list will not be used. The guide excludes new tropical hardwood (reclaimed material may be considered with evidence), virgin acrylic, PVC, neoprene and animal-derived products, and treats MDF/HDF as prohibited unless Lush expressly approves it. Every selected product must still satisfy Nigerian fire, health-and-safety, commercial-use and landlord requirements.

04. DELIVERY STRATEGY & CONTROLS
1. Local first. Use Nigerian fabrication and locally available certified systems for glass, steelwork, partitions, ceilings, screeds, adhesives, tile procurement, furniture and installation.
2. Import early. Import the Lush-approved timber boards, worktop sheets, specialist paint, Prolight lighting and recycled display materials where local alternatives cannot demonstrate compliance. The guide’s examples include 12 weeks for Metsä timber, 8 weeks for Mosa tile and 2–4 weeks for glass; obtain live quotations before fixing the programme.
3. Control every substitution. No substitute is to be purchased until Lush approves the sample and the supplier provides country of origin, EPD/recycled-content data, FSC/PEFC claim where relevant, VOC/SDS, fire/slip performance, warranty and take-back information. Imports must be planned with SONCAP and customs requirements.
4. Confirm the project inputs. Before detailed design, obtain the store area/layout, opening date, budget, landlord’s current Jabi Mall fit-out manual, fire/MEP requirements and final Lush finish approvals.
05. CONCLUSION
DOVA Futures Limited considers the proposed Lush store at Jabi Mall, Abuja achievable in principle. Nigerian suppliers and contractors can deliver much of the fabrication and installation, while selected brand-critical materials can be imported with sufficient lead time. The next stage is to confirm the mall and design information, obtain samples and certificates, agree any substitutions with Lush, and issue a detailed procurement and programme schedule. This draft is a preliminary feasibility assessment and should not be treated as a final BOQ, quotation or unconditional delivery guarantee.
