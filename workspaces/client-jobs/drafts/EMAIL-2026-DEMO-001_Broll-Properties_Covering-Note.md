## Notes for the principal — read before sending

1. **The invoice is attached on your instruction, but it precedes acceptance.**
   `RPT-2026-DEMO-001` has not been accepted, no job code has been issued, and
   the workspace rule is not to invoice before acceptance (the same rule is
   recorded against the ROAD job). Sending a ₦6,930,000 invoice alongside the
   quotation asks the client to pay against a contract they have not yet signed,
   which is unusual and can read as presumptuous. The conventional sequence is
   quotation → signed acceptance → mobilisation invoice. If you would rather
   follow that, remove the invoice attachment and the sentence "Our invoice for
   the contract, showing that schedule, is attached", and send the invoice on
   acceptance.
2. **No recipient is on file.** There is no contact name, title or email address
   for Broll Properties anywhere in the repository. "Dear Sir/Madam" is used
   because of that — replace it with the contact's name and title if you have
   them.
3. **VAT is stated at 5%** in both attachments. Nigeria's standard rate has been
   7.5% since the Finance Act 2019. This remains unconfirmed.
4. **On acceptance:** issue job code `DFL-2026-DEMO-001`, append Seq 003 to
   `bim-standards/registers/project-register.csv`, create the job folder, and
   move both documents out of `drafts/` into its `01-Documents/`.
