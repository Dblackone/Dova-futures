Motivation for the Supply and Installation of a Fire Detection and Alarm System

Ikeja City Mall — procurement recommendation following tender clarification and revised submissions

1. Purpose

This motivation supports the procurement of a complete, functional and maintainable addressable fire detection and alarm system. It records the need for the work, the outcome of the revised quotation review and the recommended order of the three shortlisted contractors.

2. Background and Need

The existing fire alarm installation has been reported as damaged and non-functional. In its present condition, it cannot be relied upon to provide effective fire detection, alarm initiation, occupant notification or system supervision. Restoration of a dependable system is therefore necessary for life safety, statutory compliance, protection of property and continuity of mall operations.

The initial request contemplated repair and upgrading of the existing GENT system. Following the first evaluation, the bidders were asked to clarify their equipment, system architecture, professional charges, certification, warranty and commercial terms. The revised submissions were reviewed together with the technical information provided.

3. Evaluation Considerations

The revised submissions were considered against the following factors:

  • the adequacy of the proposed technical solution;
  • evidence that the proposed work addresses the condition of the existing system;
  • equipment support, warranty and maintenance arrangements;
  • completeness of the installation, testing, commissioning, training and handover scope;
  • commercial terms, execution period and total cost; and
  • the contractor's ability to provide a sustainable system after installation.

4. Recommended Ranking

1

Roundbox Solutions Services — ₦42,734,219.44 including VAT

Roundbox is recommended as the first-ranked contractor. Its proposal is based on a complete replacement of the existing installation with TANDA addressable equipment. It is the only shortlisted proposal supported by two technical reports explaining why a complete change of system is proposed. The reports state that the existing GENT panels and field devices are damaged, non-functional and presently unsuitable for reliable service, and they set out the proposed TANDA panel architecture and loop capacity.

Roundbox also has a direct working relationship with TANDA, the equipment manufacturer. TANDA has committed to provide technical support, facilities training and ongoing maintenance assistance. Roundbox's quotation states a three-year warranty on materials, while TANDA's technical submission separately confirms a three-year warranty. The proposed two-week execution period and the lowest complete-project price further support the ranking.

2

JML/Fadayomi — ₦76,137,950 including VAT

JML/Fadayomi is recommended as the second-ranked contractor because it submitted the lower of the two revised GENT-based prices. Its total is ₦11,664,825 below EMO-Technical's revised total. The revised quotation also corrects the earlier omission of the GENT expansion panel, making the priced scope more complete.

The ranking remains conditional. JML must state a firm equipment-delivery period and project-execution programme, and provide an accurate technical report on the condition, serviceability and remaining useful life of the existing GENT system. It must also explain why the expansion panel was omitted from its initial quotation and subsequently added to the revised quotation, stating whether the omission was an oversight or arose from a technical judgement. The final submission should also define the system architecture, warranty, payment terms, testing, commissioning and handover obligations.

3

EMO-Technical Services — ₦87,802,775 including VAT

EMO-Technical is recommended as the third-ranked contractor. Its submission provides useful commercial particulars, including a 90-day validity period, a 30-day execution programme, a 12-month warranty and milestone payment terms of 50%, 30% and 20%. However, it is the higher-priced GENT proposal.

Its ₦6,000,000 allowance for piping, trunking and containment, ₦6,000,000 installation/civil-works allowance and ₦5,000,000 professional-services charge require detailed build-ups and commercial adjustment. EMO must also provide a technical report on the existing GENT installation and the proposed intervention. In particular, it must explain why its initial quotation specified a GENT Vigilon Plus 1–4 loop panel while the revised quotation specifies a GENT Vigilon Compact Plus 2-loop panel, and demonstrate that the revised selection has adequate loop capacity, compatibility and expansion provision.

5. Warranty and Manufacturer Support

Roundbox states a warranty period of three years on materials. TANDA also states that a three-year warranty is offered and that its team will continue to provide support during maintenance. This is the longest stated material warranty among the three ranked submissions.

EMO-Technical states a 12-month warranty. JML/Fadayomi has not provided sufficiently defined item-specific warranty information and should state the equipment warranty, workmanship warranty, commencement date, exclusions and local response obligations in its final submission.

The final warranty documentation should state:

  • that the warranty commences upon successful commissioning and handover;
  • the equipment and components covered;
  • whether labour, travel and replacement costs are included;
  • applicable exclusions and required maintenance conditions;
  • response and rectification periods;
  • the party responsible for honouring the warranty locally; and
  • the availability of critical spare parts and technical support.

6. Motivation for the Preferred Recommendation

Roundbox offers the strongest overall basis for award because its proposal combines a stated technical reason for changing the failed installation, a complete new-system approach, direct access to the TANDA manufacturer, manufacturer-backed support, the longest stated material warranty and the lowest evaluated complete-project price.

The two supporting technical reports align with the reported site condition: the existing fire alarm installation is not functioning as required, cannot presently be relied upon and is not suitable for continued use in its present state. The recommendation is therefore not based on price alone. The principal consideration is the restoration of dependable fire detection capability and the availability of a support chain for the new system after installation.

If the decision is instead to retain and overhaul the GENT system, neither JML nor EMO should proceed on quotation information alone. Each contractor should first submit an evidence-based technical condition report identifying the panels and field devices tested, recorded faults, loop condition and loading, reusable components, replacement requirements, proposed architecture, cause-and-effect implications and the technical basis for retaining GENT.

7. Conditions Before Award

  1. Formally approve the change from a GENT-compatible overhaul to a complete TANDA system replacement.
  2. Require Roundbox and TANDA to confirm the final design, loop-loading calculations, cause-and-effect matrix, device schedule, cable routes, interfaces and applicable compliance certificates before mobilisation.
  3. Obtain a signed manufacturer support letter identifying the local support contact and confirming commissioning support, training, spare-parts availability and maintenance assistance.
  4. Incorporate the three-year material warranty and a minimum 12-month workmanship warranty into the contract.
  5. Confirm that the contract price includes equipment, freight, clearing, taxes, local delivery, installation, containment, civil works, programming, testing, commissioning, training, documentation and handover.
  6. Revise the proposed payment terms so that a meaningful balance is retained until successful commissioning and handover. Any advance for imported materials should be supported by appropriate security and documentary evidence.
  7. Require test certificates, as-built drawings, operating manuals, an asset register and training records at handover.
  8. If the GENT-retention option is to remain under consideration, require both JML and EMO to submit accurate technical condition reports on the existing GENT system before any award decision.
  9. Require JML to provide a firm delivery date and execution timeline, explain the initial omission and later addition of the expansion panel, and define its warranty, payment, testing and handover terms.
  10. Require EMO to provide detailed build-ups and a revised commercial position for piping, trunking, containment, installation/civil works and professional services, and to explain the change from the initially quoted GENT Vigilon Plus 1–4 loop panel to the revised GENT Vigilon Compact Plus 2-loop panel.
  11. If Roundbox cannot satisfy the stated award conditions, JML/Fadayomi should be considered as the second-ranked reserve contractor, subject to satisfactory clarification. EMO-Technical remains the third-ranked option, also subject to satisfactory technical and commercial clarification.

8. Conclusion

Approval is recommended for the supply and installation of the fire detection and alarm system, with Roundbox Solutions Services as the preferred contractor, JML/Fadayomi as the second-ranked contractor on the basis of its lower GENT-system price, and EMO-Technical Services as the third-ranked contractor. The preferred recommendation should proceed only after the change in system basis has been formally accepted and the technical, commercial and warranty safeguards stated above have been incorporated into the award. If the GENT system is to be retained, no award should be made to JML or EMO until the required technical condition reports and contractor-specific clarifications have been satisfactorily reviewed.

This document records the procurement motivation and recommendation only. It does not constitute a contract award, technical certification or independent condition assessment.
