1.0   PROJECT OVERVIEW
This report presents the scope of works, method statement, quantity basis and quotation for the demolition and removal of existing floor tiles at Ikeja City Mall, Ikeja, Lagos, over an area of 5,000 – 5,500 m², together with the carting away and lawful disposal of all resulting tiles and rubble. The objective of the works is to strip the existing floor finish and its bedding down to a clean, exposed structural ground slab, ready to receive a new finish under a separate package.
The works are executed by mechanical breaking using heavy duty floor scrapers, supported by manual labour for scraping back to the slab, gathering and loading. Debris is removed from site progressively in 20-ton tipper trucks to an approved disposal site. The mall is a live, trading retail environment, so the works proceed bay by bay within the Client's permitted working hours, with tenant access and emergency egress maintained at all times.

2.0   SCOPE OF WORKS
· Demolition: mechanical breaking and removal of existing floor tiles and their bedding / screed over 5,000 – 5,500 m², using heavy duty floor scrapers.
· Scraping to slab: manual scraping and chipping back of residual bedding and adhesive to leave a clean, exposed structural ground slab.
· Gathering & loading: collection of arisings into stockpiles at the designated loading point and loading into tippers.
· Carting away: haulage of all tiles and rubble off site in 20-ton tipper trucks, and lawful tipping at an approved disposal site.
· Clean-down: sweeping and clearing of the exposed slab and the access routes used, on completion of each phase.

6.0   COMMERCIAL TERMS & EXCLUSIONS
· Payment schedule: 60% on mobilisation (₦4,158,000) · 30% on completion of the demolition — all tile and bedding broken out and scraped off the ground (₦2,079,000) · 10% on completion of the whole of the works, clean-down and joint sign-off (₦693,000).
· Adjustments arising under Section 7.0 are settled against the final 10% payment — a refund is set off against it, and where the refund exceeds that payment the balance is remitted to the Client within 14 days of the final account; additional trips are added to it and invoiced with the final account.
· Programme: approximately 5 – 7 weeks from mobilisation and possession of the area, subject to the Client's confirmed permitted working hours and uninterrupted access. A phased handover of the area will extend this.
· Quotation validity: 30 days from the date of this document.
· Measurement: Section A is a lump sum and is not remeasured within the 5,000 – 5,500 m² range; area beyond 5,500 m² is a variation. Section B is remeasured against signed trip tickets, each countersigned by the Client's representative, and is adjusted in either direction at ₦105,000 per trip inclusive of VAT.
· Insurance: Contractor's all-risk and workmen's compensation cover in place for the duration of the works.
· Excluded — buried services: scanning / detection is excluded and recommended as a separate item, as is repair of any service struck where no record was provided.
· Excluded — making good: repair, levelling, screeding, waterproofing or making good of the exposed structural slab. This quotation covers demolition and removal only.
· Excluded — structural & fit-out elements: removal of any part of the structural slab itself, saw-cutting, coring, or breaking out of kerbs, thresholds, plinths or shop fit-out elements, unless separately instructed.
7.0   NOTE — CARTING QUANTITY & REMEASUREMENT
· Section B is priced on 30 trips and remeasured. The Client is charged for the trips actually executed, each evidenced by a signed trip ticket countersigned by the Client's representative, at ₦105,000 per trip inclusive of VAT.
· Below 30 trips the difference is refunded to the Client; above 30 trips it is charged to the Client, at that same rate. Only trips actually executed and ticketed are billed.
· Section A is likewise a lump sum across 5,000 – 5,500 m² and carries no adjustment within that range, in either direction. Only floor area in excess of 5,500 m² gives rise to a variation, priced and agreed in writing before that work proceeds.
