1.0   PROJECT OVERVIEW
This report presents the scope of works, method statement, quantity basis and quotation for the demolition and removal of existing floor tiles at Ikeja City Mall, Ikeja, Lagos, over an area of approximately 5,000 m², together with the carting away and lawful disposal of all resulting tiles and rubble. The objective of the works is to strip the existing floor finish and its bedding down to a clean, exposed structural ground slab, ready to receive a new finish under a separate package.
The works are executed by mechanical breaking using jack hammers and scrapers, supported by manual labour for scraping back to the slab, gathering and loading. Debris is removed from site progressively in 20-ton tipper trucks to an approved disposal site. Because the mall is a live, trading retail environment, the programme, working hours and protection measures set out in Section 6.0 are integral to this quotation.

1.1   EXISTING CONDITION & EXTENT
· Existing finish: floor tiles laid on a cement sand bedding / screed over the structural ground slab.
· Required end state: bedding and screed removed in full so that the structural ground slab is exposed, swept clean and free of loose material.
· Area to be treated: approximately 5,000 m², to be confirmed by joint measurement on site before commencement.
· Setting: an operating shopping mall — works are subject to noise, dust, vibration and access controls (Section 6.0).

2.0   SCOPE OF WORKS
· Demolition: mechanical breaking and removal of existing floor tiles and their bedding / screed over approximately 5,000 m², using jack hammers and scrapers.
· Scraping to slab: manual scraping and chipping back of residual bedding and adhesive to leave a clean, exposed structural ground slab.
· Gathering & loading: collection of arisings into stockpiles at the designated loading point and loading into tippers.
· Carting away: haulage of all tiles and rubble off site in 20-ton tipper trucks, and lawful tipping at an approved disposal site.
· Clean-down: sweeping and clearing of the exposed slab and the access routes used, on completion of each phase.

6.0   WORKING IN AN OPERATING MALL — KEY CONSIDERATIONS
Ikeja City Mall trades while these works proceed. The following controls are allowed for in the rates and are integral to the programme:
· Working hours. Jack-hammer breaking is noisy and transmits vibration to adjoining tenancies. Breaking is therefore programmed for the Client's permitted hours — typically after trading. Confirmation of these hours is required before the programme in Section 8.0 can be fixed.
· Dust control. Dust screens and hoarding to shopfronts and circulation routes, water suppression at the breaking face, and covered wheeling routes. Operatives wear respiratory protection appropriate to silica dust.
· Vibration & adjacent property. Breaking is kept away from shopfront glazing, tiled feature walls and service risers; a condition record of adjoining finishes is agreed with the Client before work starts.
· Buried services. Conduits and pipework are commonly cast into screed. Where records exist they are marked up; where they do not, a scan of the area is recommended (Section 7.0). Any service struck is stopped, isolated and reported before work resumes.
· Access & egress. Emergency exits, fire routes and tenant access are kept clear at all times; tipper movements use the designated loading bay within the Client's agreed truck window.
· Safety. Full PPE, tool-box talks, barricading of open bays, and signage. All operatives are covered under the Contractor's workmen's compensation arrangements.
7.0   ASSUMPTIONS & EXCLUSIONS
· Area of 5,000 m² is approximate and taken from the brief; the final account is remeasured against joint site measurement.
· Tile and bedding thickness is assumed at 43 – 50 mm, which sets the 25 – 30 trip range in Section 4.0. Where the bedding proves a heavily bonded topping requiring materially greater breaking effort, the rate in Section A is subject to review; the trip count in Section B is capped and carries no upward adjustment (Section 9.0).
· The rates assume the Client's permitted working hours allow continuous working within each shift. Extended stop-start restrictions, or a requirement to work in short isolated windows, will require a programme and cost review.
· Scanning / detection of buried services is excluded and recommended as a separate item. Repair of services struck where no record was provided is excluded.
· Repair, levelling, screeding, waterproofing or making good of the exposed structural slab is excluded — this quotation covers demolition and removal only.
· Removal of any part of the structural slab itself, saw-cutting, coring, or breaking out of kerbs, thresholds, plinths or shop fit-out elements is excluded unless separately instructed.
· Hazardous materials are not anticipated; should any be encountered, work stops and their handling and disposal are treated as a separate instructed item.
· Statutory permits, mall access fees and tenant compensation (if any) are to the Client's account.
· Disposal is to an approved site; abnormal tipping levies imposed after the date of this quotation are recoverable at cost.

· Quotation validity: 30 days from the date of this document.
· Measurement: Section A is remeasured on the actual area treated; Section B is refunded down against signed trip tickets, each countersigned by the Client's representative, and is not adjustable upwards.
· Insurance: Contractor's all-risk and workmen's compensation cover in place for the duration of the works.
9.0   NOTE — CAPPED CARTING QUANTITY & REFUND
· The carting-away trip count cannot be fixed before the floor is opened — the true bedding thickness and the bulking of the arisings are only known once breaking begins.
· Rather than carry this as an open provisional sum, Section B is priced at the maximum of 30 trips. The Client is charged only for trips actually executed, each evidenced by a signed trip ticket, and every trip not executed is refunded at ₦107,500 inclusive of VAT.
· The trial strip described in Section 2.1 will be carried out in the first days on site, and a confirmed trip forecast issued to the Client before bulk removal proceeds.
· The Contract Sum of ₦7,256,250 is therefore a ceiling. On the 25 – 30 trip range the final account falls between ₦6,718,750 and ₦7,256,250 inclusive of VAT (Section 4.1). It cannot rise above the Contract Sum.
· Should the bedding prove materially thicker than the 43 – 50 mm assumed — taking the requirement beyond 30 trips — the additional trips are to the Contractor's account and are not chargeable to the Client.
