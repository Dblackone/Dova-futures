01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report records the visible inspection findings and proposed remedial approach for Broll Properties at Ikeja City Mall. The principal suspected route is at the roof-edge coping, blockwork, fixing penetrations and associated cracked wall; Measure 1 therefore leads the recommendation. Measure 2 is a precautionary localised treatment at the slab-to-wall perimeter. The exact entry point and path of water remain subject to opening-up and investigation when the works begin.
02. BACKGROUND
Visible water damage and cracking were observed at a roof-edge wall beneath the existing metal cap/coping, with previous fixing points and service penetrations present. The current assessment is based solely on visible inspection. The working proposal is to address the likely coping/blockwork leakage route first, then provide localised rescreeding and refelting at the slab-to-wall junction as a precaution. The attached wall image records the observed condition and is not, by itself, a definitive diagnosis.
03. FINDINGS
01 The wall shows extensive cracking, local surface failure, staining and damp-related deterioration beneath the roof-edge cap.
02 The coping/blockwork interface, existing penetrations and defective roof-cap details are the leading visible indicators of a possible water-entry route.
03 The exact leakage path and the mechanism behind the wall cracking remain unconfirmed until the affected areas are opened, cleaned and investigated during the works.
ATTACHED SITE IMAGE
Figure 1. Defective wall observed during visible inspection. This image records visible condition only and does not establish the definitive leakage path or structural cause.
04. RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Begin with Measure 1: expose and prepare the coping/blockwork, seal penetrations, investigate and repair the cracked wall, then reinstate the local waterproofing and continuous cap/coping. 2. Treat Measure 2 as a precautionary perimeter intervention: rescreed and refelt the slab-to-wall junction after confirming the measured extent on site. 3. During opening-up, trace the active water route and revise the repair sequence if the observed conditions identify a different cause. 4. Monitor the roof after completion before considering full-roof screeding.
05. CONCLUSION
The visible evidence supports starting with the coping, blockwork and cracked-wall repairs in Measure 1, with Measure 2 retained as precautionary protection at the slab-to-wall junction. This report is a pre-work assessment, not a final diagnosis: the exact cause and path of leakage can only be confirmed with greater confidence once the work opens up the affected areas. Quantities, method and cost remain subject to site measurement and principal approval.
