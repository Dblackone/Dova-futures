01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report summarises the visible inspection findings and the proposed remedial approach for Broll Properties at Ikeja City Mall. The roof-edge coping, adjacent blockwork, fixing penetrations and associated cracked wall are the leading visible indicators of a possible water-entry route. Measure 1 therefore leads the proposed works. Measure 2 is retained as a precautionary localised treatment at the slab-to-wall perimeter. The exact entry point and path of water remain subject to opening-up and investigation when the works begin.
02. BACKGROUND
Visible water damage and cracking were observed at a roof-edge wall beneath the existing metal cap/coping, with previous fixing points and service penetrations present. The current assessment is based solely on visible inspection. The working proposal is to address the likely coping/blockwork leakage route first, then provide localised rescreeding and refelting at the slab-to-wall junction as a precaution. The attached wall image records the observed condition and is not, by itself, a definitive diagnosis.
03. FINDINGS
01 The wall shows extensive cracking, local surface failure, staining and damp-related deterioration beneath the roof-edge cap.
02 The coping/blockwork interface, existing penetrations and defective roof-cap details are the leading visible indicators of a possible water-entry route.
03 The exact leakage path and the mechanism behind the wall cracking remain unconfirmed until the affected areas are opened, cleaned and investigated during the works.
ATTACHED SITE IMAGE
Figure 1. Defective wall observed during visible inspection. This image records visible condition only and does not establish the definitive leakage path or structural cause.
04. RECOMMENDATIONS
  1. Begin with Measure 1 by exposing and preparing the coping and blockwork, sealing penetrations, investigating and repairing the cracked wall, then reinstating the local waterproofing and continuous roof cap/coping.
  2. Treat Measure 2 as a precautionary perimeter intervention: rescreed and refelt the slab-to-wall junction after confirming the measured extent on site.
  3. During opening-up, trace the active water route and revise the repair sequence if the observed conditions identify a different cause.
  4. Monitor the roof after completion before considering any full-roof screeding.
05. CONCLUSION
The findings and proposed measures are based on visible evidence only. Roof leakage can follow concealed or secondary paths, and opening-up may reveal another route by which water is penetrating. If that occurs, DOVA Futures will record the finding, inform Broll Properties and agree any necessary change to the repair sequence and scope before proceeding. Quantities, method and cost remain subject to site measurement and principal approval.
