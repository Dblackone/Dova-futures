01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
A structural inspection of the reinforced concrete frame and roof to the Aterin Heights Clubhouse was carried out on 12 June 2026, ahead of Milestone 2 certification under Contract DOVA-CON-2026-ATH. The frame and roof are complete and, subject to the two remedial items recorded below, conform to the approved structural drawings and to the specified concrete grade. Twenty-eight-day cube results returned 32.4 N/mm² against a specified C25/30, with no result below 29.6 N/mm². We recommend that Milestone 2 be certified for payment. Blockwork is running eight percentage points behind programme and requires a recovery plan before the next reporting period.
02. BACKGROUND
DOVA Futures Limited was appointed by Aterin Heights Limited on 2 February 2026 for the structural completion and fit-out of the Phase 1 clubhouse and pool deck at Plot 14, Ogombo Road, Ajah, Lagos State, following acceptance of quotation QUO-2026-007. Works commenced on 9 February 2026. The contract payment schedule defines four milestones; Milestone 1 (preliminaries and substructure) was certified and paid on 12 May 2026. This inspection covers Milestone 2 — the reinforced concrete frame to first-floor level and the roof structure and covering — and was attended by the Project Manager, the Site Supervisor and the consultant architect.
03. FINDINGS
01 The reinforced concrete frame — 24 columns, ring beams and the 180 mm first-floor suspended slab — is complete over the full 480 m² footprint. Setting-out was checked against grid lines A–F and 1–5; the maximum deviation recorded was 11 mm at grid E/2, within the 15 mm tolerance stated in the specification.
02 Concrete cube tests for the suspended slab pour of 21 May 2026 returned a 28-day mean compressive strength of 32.4 N/mm², with individual results of 29.6, 33.1 and 34.5 N/mm². The specified grade is C25/30. Certificates from the testing laboratory are held in the site file.
03 Cover to the main reinforcement at column C/4 was measured at 22 mm against a specified 40 mm over a length of approximately 600 mm. The affected face was hacked back and rebuilt with a proprietary polymer-modified repair mortar on 10 June 2026 and re-measured at 41 mm. No further cover deficiencies were found in a subsequent survey of all 24 columns.
04 The roof is complete: fabricated steel trusses at 1,200 mm centres, purlins, and 360 m² of stone-coated steel sheeting. Valley flashing at the junction of the main roof and the pool-deck canopy was installed with a 90 mm upstand against a specified 150 mm. The subcontractor has been instructed to reform the flashing before the next reporting period; this is recorded as an outstanding item and does not affect Milestone 2 certification.
05 Blockwork to the external walls stands at 82% against a programme figure of 90%. The shortfall is attributable to two masonry gangs being released to another site between 25 May and 5 June 2026. Internal finishes, which follow blockwork, are correspondingly at 12% against 20%.
04. RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Certify Milestone 2 for payment in the sum of ₦29,520,000 exclusive of VAT. Owner: Contracts Manager. By 20 June 2026.
2. Reform the valley flashing to a 150 mm upstand and re-inspect before the roof is closed by the ceiling contractor. Owner: Site Supervisor. By 30 June 2026.
3. Return both masonry gangs to site and work a six-day week until blockwork is back on programme. Owner: Project Manager. By 24 July 2026.
4. Retain the column C/4 repair record and the cover survey in the handover file for inclusion in the completion documentation. Owner: Project Manager. At handover.
05. CONCLUSION
The structural frame and roof are complete and fit for the works that follow. The two defects identified were limited in extent, and one has already been remedied and verified. Milestone 2 may be certified. The programme risk on this project is no longer structural but sits in the blockwork and finishes sequence, and is addressed in recommendation 3. This report is based on visual inspection, dimensional survey and laboratory cube certificates; no opening-up or load testing was carried out.
