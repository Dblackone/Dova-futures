SUBJECT
Recovery programme for blockwork and internal finishes
REF
DOVA/MEMO/2026/018
This memorandum sets out the recovery programme agreed at the site meeting of 9 July 2026 and follows the June progress report (period ending 30 June 2026) issued to the client on 8 July 2026. Blockwork on the Aterin Heights clubhouse stands at 82% against a programme figure of 90%, and internal finishes at 12% against 20%. Overall value-weighted progress is 69.7% against a programme figure of 72.1%.
The shortfall arose when two masonry gangs were released to another site between 25 May and 5 June 2026. Both gangs returned on 6 July. From Monday 13 July the site will work a six-day week, Monday to Saturday, 07:00 to 18:00, until blockwork reaches 100%. Weekend working is authorised to 25 July 2026 only and will not be extended without the Contracts Manager's written approval, as it carries an additional preliminaries cost that is not recoverable from the client.
Internal finishes follow blockwork on a room-by-room basis rather than floor-by-floor, so that the ground-floor lounge and changing rooms can be released to the tiling gang while first-floor walling continues. The programme target remains practical completion on 18 September 2026; nothing in this memorandum varies that date or the contract sum.
