01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Overall value-weighted progress at 30 June 2026 is 69.7% against a programme figure of 72.1% — a slip of 2.4 percentage points. Milestone 2, comprising the reinforced concrete frame and the roof, was certified on 18 June 2026 following the structural inspection recorded in RPT-2026-011, and invoiced on 22 June 2026 under INV-2026-014. The slip sits entirely in blockwork, which stands at 82% against a planned 90%, and in the internal finishes that follow it. The cause was the release of two masonry gangs to another site between 25 May and 5 June 2026; both returned on 6 July and a six-day working week has been instructed to 25 July. Variation VO-01, upgrading the pool-deck balustrade to 316 stainless steel, was approved on 26 June 2026 in the sum of ₦1,840,000 exclusive of VAT. The completion date of 18 September 2026 is unchanged and no extension of time is sought.
