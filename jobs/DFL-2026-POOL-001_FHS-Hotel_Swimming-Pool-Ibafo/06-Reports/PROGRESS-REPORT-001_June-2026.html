01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Works on the swimming pool at FHS Hotel, Ibafo are progressing satisfactorily. Substructure activities — site excavation, concrete blinding and 9-inch blockwork for the pool walls — have been completed in full. Steel reinforcement bar placement is currently underway and is approximately 70% complete, with MEP conduit installation running concurrently at approximately 50%. The pool shell is taking clear form on site and the project remains broadly on programme. Casting of the base slab is targeted for the coming week, subject to supply of the client-furnished waterproofing agent, which is required before concrete is placed.
