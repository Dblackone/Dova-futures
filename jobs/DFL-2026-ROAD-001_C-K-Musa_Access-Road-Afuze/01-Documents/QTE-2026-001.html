PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Reinstatement of the access road serving Mr. C. K. Musa's residence at Afuze, Edo State, following erosion damage that has cut the road and disrupted access to the property. The proposed works comprise: (i) cutting and levelling — cutting suitable material from the existing higher terrain along the road corridor and re-using it as engineered fill, to reduce the volume of sand that must be procured; (ii) supply and delivery of additional filling sand to make up the balance, placed and compacted in layers up to stable ground level; (iii) a sandcrete blockwork-lined drain (50m) along the initial/head section of the drainage line, where flows are most erosive, to arrest further scour; (iv) an unlined earth drain (50m) continuing from the blockwork-lined section to the existing expressway drain; and (v) a concrete or interlocking-block running surface over the filled sections, extending to firm, stable ground on either side. Rates for Items 1–4 are confirmed below; the pavement area for Items 5/6 is pending a topographic/site survey.
