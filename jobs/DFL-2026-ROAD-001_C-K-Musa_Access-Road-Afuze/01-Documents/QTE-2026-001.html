PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Reinstatement of the access road serving Mr. C. K. Musa's residence at Afuze, Edo State, following erosion damage that has cut the road and disrupted access to the property. The proposed works comprise: (i) cutting and levelling — cutting suitable material from the existing higher terrain along the road corridor and re-using it as engineered fill, to reduce the volume of sand that must be procured; (ii) supply and delivery of additional filling sand to make up the balance, placed and compacted in layers up to stable ground level; (iii) a cross-road drainage channel, blockwork-lined and fitted with a perforated metal cover, to intercept oncoming erosion at the head of the works; (iv) an earth drain continuing from the end of the levelled area to the existing roadside drain; and (v) a concrete or interlocking-block running surface over the filled sections, extending to firm, stable ground on either side, over a proposed 200m² area. Rates and quantities for Items 1–4 are confirmed below; two Grand Totals are shown, one per pavement alternate, pending the client's final choice.
WHY THE DRAINAGE SYSTEM IS NEEDED
Filling and levelling the road on its own will not stop the erosion that caused the failure in the first place — without a way to safely carry off future runoff, the same water flow that washed the road out would, over time, wash away the new sand fill and undermine the finished floor. The drainage works are designed in two stages to prevent this:
  1. Cross-road channel drain (Item 3): a blockwork-lined channel built across the road at the head of the works, covered with a perforated metal grating so traffic can still pass over it. It catches the oncoming erosion/storm water before it can spread across the new fill, and carries it down the length of the levelled area in a controlled channel.
  2. Earth drain (Item 4): at the end of the levelled area, the channel transitions into a dug earth drain that ties into the existing earth drain running down the road, which ultimately discharges into the expressway drain — taking the water safely off-site.
