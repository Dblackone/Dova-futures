# Hall of Worship, Osogbo — Scope of Works Report

**Drawings referenced:** OSOGBO_POW_LONG_SEC (Longitudinal Section SO-1), OSOGBO_POW_PLAN (Ground Floor Plan), OSOGBO_POW_ALTAR_ELEV (Section S-02, Altar Elevation), plus site notes and marked-up plan extracts.
**Scope:** description of works only — no pricing. **Items below are numbered in construction sequence, not in the original job list order.**

---

## 1. Site Condition — Read Before Setting Out

A discrepancy was found between the drawings and on-site measurement along the Lobby–Altar axis:

| | Plan | On-site | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Altar wall to Lobby wall | 15.840 m | 15.246 m | −0.594 m (≈0.6 m short) |
| Altar-side door opening | 3.450 m | 3.000 m | −0.450 m |

The building as built is about 0.6 m shorter than drawn along this axis, and the altar-side door opening is correspondingly narrower on site than drawn. This zone covers the Fore Hall, Main Hall and Altar Area — i.e. it touches Items 1, 3, 4 and 5 below. It should be resolved by survey before setting out or ordering material for those items.

---

## 2. Construction Sequence

The works are to proceed in the following order:

1. **Altar steps (Item 1):** remove the existing steps; mark out the new/corrected step positions.
2. **Door impact check:** if the new step layout compromises the existing side doors to the Flower Room / Ante-Room, break down and relocate the affected door, which also requires removing the window cell (glazing) on both sides. If the doors are not compromised, proceed straight to building the new steps.
3. **Outer wall expansion at Flower Room + Ante-Room (Item 2):** excavate and cast new foundation (2 No. columns with pad footing at the building edge) → once cured, break out the existing wall being removed → the existing structural columns inside that wall are **not** to be broken until a temporary support column has been built to carry the roof/roof-slab load.
4. **Once the altar steps are properly complete:** commence ceiling and drywall works.
5. **Ceiling first (Item 3),** then the drywall partition (Item 4) and the curved altar wall (Item 5) proceed together.

---

## 3. Item 1 — Correction to Altar Steps

**Scope:**
1. Remove the existing (incorrectly positioned) altar steps
2. Mark out and rebuild new, corrected steps to match the design intent
3. **Conditional:** if the new step footprint compromises the existing side doors to the Flower Room / Ante-Room — plausible given the 3.0 m vs 3.45 m door discrepancy in Section 1 — break down and relocate the affected door, and remove the window cell (glazing) on both sides. If the doors are not compromised, this extra work does not apply.

**Outstanding for this item:** a site inspection is needed to determine which of the two scenarios (door compromised / not compromised) actually applies, since it changes the extent of the work.

---

## 4. Item 2 — Structural Changes to Side Walls (Outer Wall Expansion, Flower Room + Ante-Room)

**Scope:** this is a foundation, column and demolition package, not a simple wall replacement.

1. Excavate and cast a new RC foundation for the expanded outer wall
2. 2 No. columns with pad footing at the building edge, on the new foundation
3. Once the foundation has cured, break out the existing wall being removed
4. **Temporary works requirement:** the existing structural columns embedded in that wall are not to be broken until a temporary support column has been built to carry the roof and roof-slab load during the works — this is a shoring/needling step, required before the permanent columns can be cut out

**Outstanding for this item:** dimensioned structural/civil drawing for the wall expansion (length, foundation depth/width, footing size), and a temporary-works method statement from a structural engineer specifying the shoring column design (size, spacing, bearing capacity) needed to safely support the roof during demolition. This should be treated as a design deliverable before construction starts, not left to be worked out on site.

---

## 5. Item 3 — Ceiling Installation (Drywall), Two Stages

1. **Stage 1 — Fore Hall + Main Hall (seating area):** flat drywall ceiling, installed first, before the Item 4 partition goes in
2. **Stage 2 — Altar Area:** raised approximately 600mm above the Main Hall ceiling level, per the section drawing's `+600` step annotations. This stage is the same physical element as Item 5's altar gable/curved wall assembly — it is one scope, not two

**Construction:** 10mm plasterboard on 60mm steel stud & track, with wall angles and profiles at the perimeter/junctions.

**Outstanding for this item:** confirm the Fore Hall + Main Hall ceiling area on site — it sits within the same zone affected by the 0.6m discrepancy in Section 1.

---

## 6. Item 4 — New Partition Wall (Fore Hall / Main Hall)

**Location:** Gridline 6, dividing the Fore Hall from the Main Hall.

**Site condition:** this partition does not currently exist on site.

**Scope:** full-height drywall partition — this replaces the plan's original detail of 150mm blockwork to 3.0m with drywall above; the wall is now to be drywall throughout its height.

- Construction: 10mm cement board on 75mm steel stud & track, both faces
- Height: floor to the underside of the new flat ceiling (Item 3) — the ceiling is installed first, and the partition is built up to meet it
- The wall hosts a door opening; the door edges require jamb/edge reinforcement in the stud framing
- Length spans the full internal clear width of the hall between the Left and Right Corridor walls

**Outstanding for this item:** confirm the clear internal width and the ceiling height on site; confirm the door schedule (size/count) for the opening in this wall.

---

## 7. Item 5 — Altar Dry Wall (Gable Wall + Curved Wall)

Two related elements enclosing the Altar Area, built together after the ceiling (Item 3) is in.

### 5a. Gable wall
The vertical infill wall closing the end of the roof/altar canopy structure, per Section S-02: rectangular up to 5.4 m high at the sides, rising to 6.6 m at the centre peak, 9.6 m wide, cladding on both faces.
- Construction: cement board on 75mm steel stud & track (board thickness not yet confirmed — see Open Items)

### 5b. Curved wall at the altar
The curved apse wall enclosing the Altar Area, following the plan's arc geometry (R3.815–R5.096) from the Ante-Room corner around to the Flower Room corner — developed length approx. 8.4 m (two 4.2 m halves either side of the centreline), same height profile as the gable wall (5.4 m rising to 6.6 m at the crown).
- Construction: 10mm plasterboard on 60mm steel stud & track (laid to follow the curve), replacing the aluminum-profile framing shown on the architectural drawing — the field decision is steel stud/track instead

**Sequencing link to Item 2:** the drawing marks "Remove existing Block Wall" at the base of this curve, on the Flower Room end — that demolition is carried under Item 2 and must happen before Item 5b's framing/boarding goes in. The Ante-Room end of the curve has no such removal note; confirm on site whether that end is new work against existing fabric or also needs clearing.
