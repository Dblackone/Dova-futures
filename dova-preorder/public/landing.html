Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my item is out of stock in China?

If a product is confirmed unavailable from the Chinese supplier, you will receive a full refund of your goods payment within 14 working days. This is the only condition under which a refund is issued.

How is the shipping fee calculated?

The shipping fee is based on the actual weight and dimensions of your packed goods in China. We cannot give you an exact amount until your goods are packed. We will send you an invoice with the exact amount once we know.

What happens if I don't pay the shipping fee?

If you do not pay the shipping fee within 7 calendar days of being invoiced, your goods will be forfeited. Forfeited goods become property of Dova Futures and your Stage 1 (goods) payment is non-refundable.

Can I return goods after they arrive?

No. This is a preorder service, not a marketplace. All sales are final once the preorder window closes. Please review product descriptions carefully before ordering.

How long does delivery take?

Typically 6–10 weeks from the order window close date, depending on the shipping method and customs clearance. All dates on the tracking page are estimates.

How do I track my order?

Visit /track and enter your order reference number (e.g. DF-260701-0001) or your phone number. You'll see a live status timeline of your goods' journey.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed by Paystack, Nigeria's leading payment gateway. We never store your card details. Paystack supports card, bank transfer, and USSD payments.
