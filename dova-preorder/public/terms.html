1. Nature of Transaction (Preorder)

This transaction is a preorder, not a marketplace purchase. Dova Futures ("the Company") acts as a procurement agent on behalf of the buyer, not as a vendor holding existing stock.

Goods are sourced from Chinese suppliers only after the preorder window closes and all payments are received. The buyer agrees that availability cannot be guaranteed until supplier confirmation is received by the Company.

By placing an order and accepting these Terms, the buyer acknowledges the preorder nature of this service and accepts all associated risks and conditions herein.

2. Payment Stages

Payment for goods and related services occurs in up to three (3) separate stages:

  • Stage 1 — Goods Payment: Full payment of the listed goods price at checkout during the active preorder window. This payment is made in Nigerian Naira (₦) via Paystack.
  • Stage 2 — Shipping Fee: A separate fee charged after goods are packed in China and the actual weight and dimensions are confirmed. The amount varies per buyer depending on the size and weight of their specific goods. This fee is invoiced separately and is not included in the Stage 1 payment.
  • Stage 3 — Waybill Fee: The cost of delivering the buyer's goods from the Company's Akure warehouse to the buyer's stated delivery address. This is charged when the goods are ready for dispatch.
All three stages are separate payments. Completing Stage 1 does not cover shipping or waybill costs.

3. No Returns Policy

Due to the preorder nature of this service, the Company operates a strict no-returns and no-exchange policy.

All sales are final once the preorder window closes. Goods cannot be returned to the Company or to the Chinese supplier under any circumstances, as they are procured specifically based on buyer orders.

The buyer is advised to review all product descriptions, images, and specifications carefully before placing an order. The Company is not responsible for buyer dissatisfaction with a product's appearance, size, colour, or function where the product matches the listing description.

4. Refund Conditions

A full refund of the Stage 1 (Goods Payment) will be issued only under the following condition:

  • The specific product ordered by the buyer is confirmed unavailable from the Chinese supplier after the preorder window closes.

Refunds under this condition will be processed within 14 working days of the Company confirming unavailability to the buyer. Refunds will be made to the original payment method via Paystack.

No other grounds for refund exist under these Terms. In particular, the following do not constitute grounds for a refund:

  • Force majeure events — see Clause 7.

6. Waybill Fee & Storage Terms

The Stage 3 Waybill Fee covers the cost of dispatching the buyer's goods from the Company's Akure warehouse to the buyer's delivery address.

Storage at the Company's Akure warehouse is free of charge for fourteen (14) days after the buyer is notified that goods are ready for dispatch.

After 14 days, a daily storage fee will apply. The current rate will be communicated upon notification.

Goods held beyond ninety (90) days from the date of arrival at the Akure warehouse without the buyer paying the waybill fee will be treated as abandoned and forfeited. No refund of any previously paid amount will be made in such cases.

Goods prices are quoted in Nigerian Naira (₦) based on the exchange rate prevailing at the time of listing.

In the event that the Naira depreciates by more than ten percent (10%) against the Chinese Yuan (CNY) between the listing date and the currency conversion date, the Company reserves the right to issue a supplementary invoice for the difference.

Buyers who receive a forex adjustment invoice have the right to:

  • Pay the supplementary amount and proceed with their order, or
  • Decline to pay, in which case they are entitled to a full refund of their Stage 1 payment only. No other amounts will be refunded.

Buyers will be given a minimum of seven (7) days to respond to any forex adjustment invoice.
