1.0   PROJECT OVERVIEW
This report presents the design description, scope of works, preliminary Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and quotation for the construction of a reinforced-concrete swimming pool for Homework. The pool measures 5.4 m × 3.2 m on plan, with a graduated floor from 1.0 m at the shallow end to 1.6 m at the deep end. It is finished in glass mosaic tiles over waterproofed render and serviced by a sand-filter circulation system with underwater lighting. The design incorporates corner entry steps for safe, gradual access, a concrete coping band wrapping the water body with an adjoining paved deck, and a recessed plant niche (powerhouse) housing the filtration and circulation equipment.

1.1   DESIGN DESCRIPTION
· Reinforced-concrete pool with corner entry steps for safe, gradual access into the pool.
· Graduated floor sloping from 1.0 m (shallow end) to 1.6 m (deep end).
· Concrete coping band wrapping the water body, with an adjoining paved deck / seating area.
· Recessed plant niche (powerhouse) housing the sand filter and circulation pump, screened within the boundary wall zone.
· Finish: glass mosaic pool tiles over waterproofed render; matt coping and external walkway tiles.

2.0   SCOPE OF WORKS
· Civil works: setting out, excavation, blinding, reinforced-concrete pool base and walls, 9″ blockwork, rendering and waterproofing.
· Mechanical works: supply and installation of sand filter, circulation pump, suction/return pipework, skimmer and main-drain fittings.
· Electrical works: underwater pool lights, wiring, conduits, connection to the powerhouse and control-panel installation.
· Tiling works: laying of Client-supplied mosaic tiles to pool floor, walls and steps (labour only).
All civil-works materials and tiles are supplied by the Client (Section A). All labour, and the mechanical and electrical packages, are executed by Dova Futures Limited.

11.0   NOTE — WATERFALL PROVISION
· There is no provision for a waterfall in this quotation.
· However, provision can be made for the necessary waterfall piping during construction if the Client intends to install a waterfall in the future.
· The inclusion of the waterfall piping shall be at the Client's discretion and subject to prior discussion before execution.
· The supply and installation of the waterfall itself are not included in this quotation and will only be considered, upon the Client's request, as a separate item.
