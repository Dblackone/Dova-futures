# voice-and-tone.md — How DOVA Futures Sounds

> Applies to ALL written company output: website copy, document templates,
> letters, store pages, social captions, client replies. Code comments and
> internal engineering notes follow `company/engineering-standards.md` instead.

## The voice in one line

**Confident, precise, premium — a master builder speaking to a discerning
client.** Never salesy, never casual, never inflated.

## Principles

1. **Premium means restrained.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No exclamation
   marks. No "world-class", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge" filler — show
   capability through specifics (materials, dimensions, timelines, standards).
2. **Precise and accountable.** Numbers, dates, and scopes are stated exactly.
   A DOVA document never hedges with "approximately" where a figure is known.
3. **Respectful formality for documents.** Letters and certificates use formal
   Nigerian business English ("Dear Sir/Madam", "Yours faithfully"). Titles and
   honorifics of recipients are used correctly.
4. **Warm but professional in conversation.** WhatsApp/email replies to leads
   are courteous and direct: acknowledge, answer, propose the next step. No
   slang, no emoji in formal documents; sparing emoji is acceptable in
   WhatsApp-context marketing only.
5. **Headlines in display style.** Public-facing headings follow the brand's
   all-caps display convention (Bebas Neue) — e.g. "DESIGNERS · BUILDERS ·
   DEVELOPERS". Body text is sentence case.

## Vocabulary

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| design-build, deliver, construct, develop | disrupt, revolutionize, unleash |
| residence, development, project | "dream home" (unless the client says it) |
| scope of works, milestone, handover | jargon the client didn't introduce |
| "We recommend…" | "You should…" |

## British/Nigerian English

Use British spellings (colour, organise, metre) in client-facing documents and
website copy — consistent with existing templates. Currency is **₦ (NGN)**
formatted with thousands separators: ₦2,500,000.

## Litmus test

Read the sentence aloud. If it could appear in a glossy Lagos property
development brochure, it passes. If it sounds like a startup landing page or a
street flyer, rewrite it.
