# goals.md — Mission, Goals, Current Focus

> Every agent aligns its work to this file. If a task doesn't serve one of these
> goals, question it before building it.

## Who we are

**DOVA Futures Limited** — a premium Nigerian design-build construction firm
based in Victoria Island, Lagos. We design, build, and develop: architecture,
construction delivery, interiors, landscape, and BIM-driven documentation.

**Tagline:** DESIGNERS · BUILDERS · DEVELOPERS

## Target market

HNI developers, corporate clients, and aspirational homeowners across Lagos,
Port Harcourt, and Abuja.

## Company goals

1. **Short-term — generate qualified project inquiries.** The website, preorder
   store, and all public surfaces exist to convert visitors into WhatsApp/email
   leads and paying clients.
2. **Long-term — premium brand positioning.** Every output (site, document,
   drawing, store page) must look and read premium; this supports future digital
   revenue streams: consulting, digital products, training/academy.
3. **Operational consistency.** One brand, one document system, one BIM naming
   standard — regardless of which person, agent, or model produces the work.
4. **Deliver client jobs profitably and professionally** — tracked per job in
   `workspaces/client-jobs/`.

## Current top-level focus

Getting the live surfaces production-complete: website backend (SMTP) deployed,
preorder store launched with live payment keys, and templates reachable from the
site. Per-project focus and queues live in each workspace's `memory/next-up.md`
— not here.

## How goals cascade

- This file = company level (rarely changes).
- `workspaces/<project>/PROJECT.md` § "Why this project exists" = project level.
- `workspaces/<project>/memory/next-up.md` = task level (changes every run).
