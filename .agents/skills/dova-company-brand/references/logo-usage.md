# DOVA Futures logo usage

The design system selects a logo by the available shape and the background. A
variant is an arrangement of the same identity, not a new logo.

| Space or need | Select | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Wide light header, document, business card or presentation | `assets/logo-variations/01-horizontal-green-on-white.png` | The full name stays readable in a horizontal space |
| Wide forest-green header, cover or banner | `assets/logo-variations/02-horizontal-cream-on-green.png` | Reversed contrast keeps the mark legible on the brand surface |
| Square tile where the company name must appear | `assets/logo-variations/04-square-stacked-cream-on-green.png` | Stacked layout uses the square area efficiently; the light stacked file remains review-only |
| Small light avatar or compact marker | `assets/logo-variations/05-symbol-green-on-white.png` | The mark survives reduction better than a long wordmark |
| Small dark avatar or app-icon artwork direction | `assets/logo-variations/06-symbol-cream-on-green.png` | Reversed symbol has clear contrast on forest green |
| Narrow space where the mark is already identified nearby | `assets/logo-variations/07-wordmark-green-on-white.png` | Keeps the company name without competing with another mark |
| Single-colour print reference | `assets/logo-variations/08-horizontal-black-on-white.png` | A simple black-and-white arrangement is easier to reproduce |

Keep the supplied aspect ratio and leave visible clear space around the artwork.
Do not stretch a square image into a banner, or put the full-name lockup into a
favicon-sized space. Use the symbol when the name would no longer be legible.
The PNGs have solid backgrounds; they are not transparent watermarks.

Google-specific export guidance is recorded in `assets/logo-variations/logo-manifest.json`:
Business Profile recommends a 720 × 720 PNG/JPG; Ads recommends a 1200 × 1200
square with important content near the centre; Performance Max uses a 4:1
horizontal logo; Search favicons are square and Google recommends larger than
48 × 48. These are export targets, not claims that an upload has been made or
accepted.

Current document templates retain the inline SVG letterhead lockup because it
includes the canonical services descriptor `DESIGNERS · BUILDERS · DEVELOPERS`.
Changing those templates is a separate pagination and document-system decision.
